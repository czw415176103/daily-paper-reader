## 摘要
碱金属硼酸盐玻璃的玻璃化转变温度（$T_{g}$）强烈依赖于组成，且由于硼网络的结构复杂性，难以从第一性原理预测。本文应用物理信息符号回归（结合进化搜索与物理意义描述符），为$x\mathrm{M}_2\mathrm{O}\cdot(100-x)\mathrm{B}_2\mathrm{O}_3$玻璃家族（M = Li, Na, K，x以mol%表示）推导出具有可解释性的$T_{g}$闭式表达式，并进一步外推至M = Rb和Cs。所得模型在保持清晰物理可解释性的同时，实现了14-16 K的均方根误差，明确捕捉了$T_{g}$、结构解离能和网络堆积之间的相互作用。关键的是，基于刚性单元堆积分数（RUPF）构建的模型比使用传统原子堆积分数（APF）的模型产生更真实的$T_{g}$预测，因为APF在中间组成处高估了结构刚性。拟合的解离能进一步与修正的Makishima-Mackenzie模型进行验证，确认推断参数在碱金属硼酸盐家族内是物理一致的，而不仅仅具有统计有效性。最后，蒙特卡洛不确定性量化显示，与硼异常相关的组成区域预测不确定性最高，直接将模型局限性与此类玻璃中已知的结构转变联系起来。该结果凸显了物理信息符号回归作为玻璃系统性质预测中黑箱模型的透明、可解释替代方案的潜力。

## Abstract
The glass transition temperature ($T_{g}$) of alkali borate glasses is strongly composition-dependent and difficult to predict from first principles due to the structural complexity of the boron network. Here, we apply physics-informed symbolic regression (combining evolutive search with physically meaningful descriptors) to derive an interpretable closed-form expression for $T_{g}$ in the $x\mathrm{M}_2\mathrm{O}\cdot(100-x)\mathrm{B}_2\mathrm{O}_3$ glass family, with M = Li, Na, and K and $x$ expressed in mol%, and subsequently extrapolate it to M = Rb and Cs. The resulting model achieves a root-mean-square error of 14-16 K while maintaining clear physical interpretability, explicitly capturing the interplay among $T_{g}$, structural dissociation energy, and network packing. Critically, models built on the Rigid Unit Packing Fraction (RUPF) yield substantially more realistic $T_{g}$ predictions than those using the conventional Atomic Packing Fraction (APF), as APF overestimates structural rigidity at intermediate compositions. The fitted dissociation energies are further validated against the revised Makishima-Mackenzie model, confirming that the inferred parameters are physically consistent, not merely statistically effective, within the alkali borate family. Finally, Monte Carlo uncertainty quantification reveals that prediction uncertainty is highest in the compositional regions associated with the boron anomaly, directly linking model limitations to a known structural transition in these glasses. This result highlights the potential of physics-informed symbolic regression as a transparent and interpretable alternative to black-box models for property prediction in glass systems.

- **方法论价值**：物理信息符号回归可作为黑箱模型之外的一种透明、可解释的玻璃性质预测途径，并能用于估计难以直接测量的物理量（如解离能）和跨性质迁移。
